## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is an ethereal pastel-mint culinary fantasy with cozy 2000s cooking-game nostalgia. Compress the subject—food or not—into one central pixel symbol on a pale ceramic plate, wrapped in a soft white halo, mint lace, tiny flowers and dot rings. Faint kitchen objects may float far back and nearly disappear into white. The centre alone has clear chunky pixel edges. Everything else is dreamy, soft, blooming and almost overexposed. Mint, ivory and cream lead; bright orange, coral, gold or pink stay on the centre. It is not a dark game UI, a sharp icon set, a filled kitchen scene, a grey filter or a noisy collage.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
